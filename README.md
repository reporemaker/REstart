## Why

Your apps answer to you, not the other way round. RE*start clears the first screen of
everything that competes for your attention and leaves a quiet, considered surface you finish
yourself: the type, the colours, the clock, the order. Fewer things, chosen well; a home screen
that carries your taste, not the noise of every app on it.

## What it does

- **Names, not icons:** up to 8 apps on the home screen, by name. No coloured icons, no badges;
  you open the phone and go where you meant to.
- **Search first:** two or three letters are enough, and a typo is forgiven; if no app matches
  it looks in files, then contacts. You find instead of browsing, and what you type stays on
  the device.
- **A drawer with categories:** everything else in groups, from your own tags or from what each
  app says it is. Straight to the right shelf instead of a long list.
- **Swiss layout, your colours:** the grid, hairline rules and clear headings come from Swiss
  typography; its red does not. Colour is kept quiet on purpose, so the screen never calls for
  you like a slot machine, and every colour from the background to the clock is yours to set.
- **A screen that looks like you:** four clock styles (Classic, Swiss, Rings, One line), your
  font on every screen, widgets that stay where you put them on a grid. Same app, a different
  screen in every hand.
- **Settings where you expect them:** three sections by what they affect: Features, Look &
  Feel, Advanced. An automatic backup before every update, 21 languages. You find what you
  look for, and an update never undoes your setup.
- **Quiet:** no ads, no analytics, no crash uploads, no accounts. The only request it makes on
  its own is the weather, with the location rounded to about 1 km; dependencies audited with
  [LifeLine](https://github.com/reporemaker/LifeLine). A home screen that does not sell your
  attention. Details in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## Install

> **APK only for now.** App store releases are coming soon.

Download the APK from the [latest release](https://github.com/reporemaker/REstart/releases/latest)
and open it on the phone. Then make RE*start your home app; on most phones: Settings → Apps →
Default apps → Home app.
